THE BEGINNING OF GARAM THANDA

This is the little origin tale behind our café — a fictional brand story imagined for Garam Thanda, inspired by the everyday warmth of Pakistani dhaba culture.

✦
01

A HUMBLE BEGINNING

The kettle that brought people together

Our story begins on a lively street, where the evening air carried the smell of fresh parathas and the sound of friends catching up. A small tea stall stood beneath a faded signboard. Its owner, an imaginative chai lover named Hamza, believed that a good cup of tea could turn strangers into friends.

He kept a kettle simmering from early morning until late at night. Students stopped by after classes, shopkeepers came for a quick break, and rickshaw drivers knew there would always be a warm cup waiting. Nobody was ever in a hurry to leave.

🫖THE FIRST KETTLE
02

WHEN TWO WORLDS MET

Garam, thanda — and everything in between

One summer, a regular customer asked for something cold to go with his spicy snacks. Another wanted a strong, steaming doodh patti. Hamza laughed and said, “Why choose? Life needs a little garam and a little thanda.” The phrase stuck.

Soon the tiny stall grew a few tables. Chai was joined by lemonade, crispy fries, halwa puri and comforting desi meals. The menu became a cheerful mix of hot favourites and cool refreshers — just like the people who gathered there.

🥤A REFRESHING IDEA
03

A TABLE FOR EVERYONE

From a roadside stop to a little dhaba dream

Years passed, but the feeling stayed the same. The stall became a cosy café-dhaba where families shared breakfasts, friends celebrated small victories, and solo visitors found a familiar corner. The sign above the door finally read Garam Thanda — a name that promised variety, comfort and a warm welcome.

Today, that same spirit lives in every plate and every pour. We may have a bigger menu, but our favourite ingredient is still the same: the joy of bringing people together.
